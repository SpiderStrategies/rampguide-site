03Questionsthe short answers
Do I need the library to use RampGuide?

Every plan includes it, and no requirement has to use a pack. The engine generates the package from whatever facts are in your repo, and the importer brings your existing prose across word for word. Packs are for the requirements where you would rather start from auditor-ready, parameterized content for the tools everyone runs, and keep it current without writing it yourself.

What happens when FedRAMP changes something?

We update the packs. Your next rampguide add re-assembles the current pack with your overrides layered on top, so your edits survive and the new language lands. Rebuild, diff dist/, and you can see exactly what moved.

Does my compliance content leave my environment?

In the hosted app, your package lives with us: plain files in a git repository we store for you, and a full copy (a git bundle you clone like any repository) is yours whenever you ask. Builds use no AI. If your boundary requires that nothing leaves, run the command line inside it instead: builds make no network calls, and the library service sees only which key downloaded which pack.

What exactly do I receive after paying?

A library key, shown once on the page right after checkout. Two lines in your shell, the library URL and the key, and rampguide add --list shows every pack with how many of your gaps it fills. Keep the key somewhere safe; we store only a hash of it and can't show it again. If you lose it, we revoke it and issue a new one.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time from the receipt Stripe emails you. The subscription runs to the end of the paid year, then stops; the key stops working when the subscription ends. What you've already installed stays in your repo. Terms and refund details are being finalized; ask us before you buy if they matter to your decision.

Is RampGuide itself FedRAMP authorized?

No. The hosted app is not FedRAMP authorized, and nothing here implies endorsement or certification by FedRAMP or the U.S. General Services Administration. If your program needs the build to stay inside your own authorization boundary, the command line runs there. Ask us what your assessor will want to know about the hosted app.
