Before you start

A few practical details.

What is a package?

One product or environment prepared against one framework. A second framework or system is a separate package in the same company.

What happens after checkout?

After payment is confirmed, the application creates your company and sends the named owner an invitation. The owner enrolls with a passkey, sets up the package and invites the team. Contact us if your invitation has not arrived.

Will this certify or authorize my product?

No. RampGuide helps prepare, maintain and share documentation. Publication is separate from an assessment, CMMC certification or FedRAMP authorization. RampGuide itself is not FedRAMP authorized.

Can I start with existing documents?

Setup supports importing the accepted FedRAMP Moderate Word Appendix A and OSCAL SSP formats. Import availability depends on your selected framework and source format; it is not a general document or evidence upload service.

What about cancellation, terms or a purchase order?

Contact us to confirm contractual terms, cancellation and refund details, or invoicing before purchasing.
