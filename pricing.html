03Questionsthe short answers
Do I need the library to use RampGuide?

No. The engine generates the package from whatever facts you put in the repo, and the importer brings your existing prose across word for word. The library is for teams who would rather start from auditor-ready, parameterized content for the tools everyone runs, and keep it current without writing it themselves.

What happens when FedRAMP changes something?

We update the packs. Your next rampguide add re-assembles the current pack with your overrides layered on top, so your edits survive and the new language lands. Rebuild, diff dist/, and you can see exactly what moved.

Does my compliance content leave my environment?

No. Builds run locally with no network and no AI. The library API only ever serves packs to you; it sees which key downloaded which pack and nothing about your repo. Your package lives in your git.

What exactly do I receive after paying?

A library key, shown once on the page right after checkout. Two lines in your shell, the library URL and the key, and rampguide add --list shows every pack with how many of your gaps it fills. Keep the key somewhere safe; we store only a hash of it and can't show it again. If you lose it, we revoke it and issue a new one.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time from the receipt Stripe emails you. The subscription runs to the end of the paid year, then stops; the key stops working when the subscription ends. What you've already installed stays in your repo. Terms and refund details are being finalized; ask us before you buy if they matter to your decision.

Is RampGuide itself FedRAMP authorized?

No, and it doesn't need to be: it's a tool you run inside your own environment, not a service that holds your data. Nothing here implies endorsement or certification by FedRAMP or the U.S. General Services Administration.
