Privacy notice

What RampGuide collects, and what it doesn't

Draft for review · placeholder effective date: TBD

This website

rampguide.com is static pages served by GitHub Pages. We run no analytics, no advertising pixels, no chat widgets, and set no cookies. GitHub's hosting logs are subject to GitHub's privacy statement.

Buying a subscription

Checkout happens on Stripe's hosted page. Stripe collects your card details, billing address, and email under Stripe's privacy policy; we never see your card number. From Stripe we receive your email address, the organization and offering names you typed on our pricing page, and the subscription and payment identifiers, and we keep them to operate your subscription and reach you about it.

The library service

When the engine downloads a pack with your key, our service records the key's hash prefix, your organization label, the pack name, and the time. That log is how we operate the service and notice abuse. We store only a hash of your key. Your content repository, statements, parameters, and compiled documents never reach us — the engine builds locally with no network access.

Email

If you email us, we keep the correspondence to answer you. We do not add you to marketing lists.

Retention and your rights

Subscription records are kept for the life of the subscription and as long afterward as tax and accounting rules require. Write to hello@rampguide.com to ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted where the law allows.

Changes

Updates to this notice are posted here with a new effective date.
