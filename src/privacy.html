Privacy notice

What RampGuide collects, and what it doesn't

Draft for review · placeholder effective date: TBD

This website

rampguide.com is static pages served by GitHub Pages. We run no analytics, no advertising pixels, no chat widgets, and set no cookies. GitHub's hosting logs are subject to GitHub's privacy statement.

Buying a subscription

The pricing page links to our contact email. If you contact us to get started, we use the information you provide to answer your questions and arrange onboarding. Online checkout is not currently offered. Payment-provider disclosures must be finalized before online payments are enabled.

The RampGuide app

The app runs at trust.rampguide.com on Amazon Web Services, in its commercial US East (N. Virginia) region. It holds:

  • People. Each person's name and email address, and the public half of their passkey. A passkey is the only way to sign in: there is no password, and the private half never leaves the person's device. Sign-in codes, recovery codes, sessions and API keys are stored only as hashes.
  • Your package. The answers your people give, the documents uploaded during setup (deleted once setup has created the package), the package's repository with each change's author and date, and every build and publish. It is encrypted at rest. Builds use no AI and send your content nowhere.
  • Your trust center's records. Requests for access (the requester's name, email, organization and stated reason), who was let in or turned down and by whom, and every view and download with the person, the file and the package version. Your owners see these records; they are the access log FedRAMP's rules ask you to keep.
  • Service logs. Each request's time, path, IP address and browser identifier, and a record of every read and write of stored packages, used to run and secure the service.

RampGuide's operators can reach this data to run the service, and can read a trust center's package at its assessor tier. They never decide who gets in. [Who the operators are, and how quickly a customer hears of an incident — leadership decision pending.]

Who else sees it

Only the people your owners let in read your package, at the tier they allow. When an owner turns down a federal agency, FedRAMP's rules have the owner report it; the app gives the owner the report's text and sends nothing to FedRAMP itself.

Email

The app sends sign-in codes, invitations and notices of new requests from rampguide.com through Amazon Simple Email Service. If you email us, we keep the correspondence to answer you. We do not add you to marketing lists.

Retention and your rights

Access records and service logs are kept for 400 days. Subscription records are kept for the life of the subscription and as long afterward as tax and accounting rules require. When a subscription ends, a full copy of your package's repository is yours on request.
