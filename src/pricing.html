Before you start

A few practical details.

What is a package?

One system prepared against one framework. A second framework or system is a separate package in the same company. Both are included in your subscription.

How do we get started?

Email us with the program you are preparing for and whether you have existing documents. We’ll confirm your starting point and arrange onboarding. Your owner signs in with a passkey and invites the team once the workspace is ready.

Will this certify or authorize my product?

No. RampGuide helps prepare, maintain and share documentation. Publication is separate from an assessment, CMMC certification or FedRAMP authorization. RampGuide itself is not FedRAMP authorized.

Can I start with existing documents?

Setup supports importing the accepted FedRAMP Moderate Word Appendix A and OSCAL SSP formats, and CMMC Level 2 self-assessment workbooks (.xlsx). Import availability depends on your selected framework and source format; it is not a general document or evidence upload service.
