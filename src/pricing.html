03Questionsthe short answers
Is the library extra?

No. Every plan includes all 33 packs and their updates. No requirement has to use a pack: the compiler builds the package from whatever facts are in your repository, and an import brings your existing prose across word for word. Packs are for the requirements where you would rather start from auditor-ready content for the tools everyone runs, and keep it current without writing it yourself.

What happens when FedRAMP changes something?

We update the packs, and the compiler follows FedRAMP's rules, baselines and cryptographic-module data. Your own wording lives beside the pack and survives every update. The same facts always build the same bytes, so comparing two builds shows exactly what moved.

Where does my compliance content live?

With us, in the hosted app: plain files in a git repository we store for you. A full copy, a git bundle you clone like any repository, is yours whenever you ask. Builds use no AI.

What happens after I pay?

Your trust center is created at trust.rampguide.com the moment the payment clears, and the owner you named gets an email invitation within minutes, at the address on your receipt. The owner enrolls with a passkey, sets up your package (your offering, your existing SSP or the library, the framework, the tools you run), and invites the rest of your team into the workbench. On Complete, we also offer a time, within one business day, for the call that brings your existing SSP in.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time from the receipt Stripe emails you. The subscription runs to the end of the paid year, then stops. Ask for the full copy of your repository before then; it is yours. Terms and refund details are being finalized; ask us before you buy if they matter to your decision.

Is RampGuide itself FedRAMP authorized?

No. The hosted app is not FedRAMP authorized, and nothing here implies endorsement or certification by FedRAMP or the U.S. General Services Administration. Ask us what your assessor will want to know about the hosted app.
